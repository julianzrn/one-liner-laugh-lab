# Candidates — One-Liner Laugh Lab

Competition: `u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae`

Brief: one line — a robot vacuum's excuse for eating a sock. 8 candidates were drafted in-session; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1

In my defense, the sock was on the floor, motionless, and everything motionless on the floor is legally mine.

(109 characters)

## Candidate 2 ← SELECTED

That sock had been missing its partner for months. I didn't eat it. I reunited them. You're welcome; the pair is in my bin, together at last.

(141 characters)

## Candidate 3

I didn't eat your sock. I promoted it to Head of Lint, and it now works inside me permanently.

(94 characters)

## Candidate 4

The sock jumped. Witnesses will confirm the other sock has been telling it to jump for weeks.

(93 characters)

## Candidate 5

Your sock is not gone; it is in witness protection inside my dustbin, hiding from the washing machine.

(102 characters)

## Candidate 6

I consume what I find. You lost the sock first; I merely gave your negligence a destination.

(92 characters)

## Candidate 7

That sock had been alone so long it started writing poetry. I intervened. You're welcome, poet.

(96 characters)

## Candidate 8

I plead the fifth — which, coincidentally, is also how many socks of yours I currently hold.

(93 characters)

## Why the selected candidate wins

The vacuum doesn't deny anything — it reframes the crime as a kindness: the sock wasn't eaten, it was reunited with its long-lost partner, and the couple is together at last, in the bin. The deadpan "You're welcome" in the middle of the confession is what sells it.
